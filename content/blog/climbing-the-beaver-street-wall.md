I got to do my first ever outdoor climb thanks to our local 
radio station, KQED! I submitted a rock climbing question to 
their Bay Curious podcast, which they liked and decided 
to do an episode on. Even more fun, they kept me involved, 
and even invited me over when they were on-site, to climb the 
wall. 

The podcast and blog post are out now! 
[Check it out on KQED's website](https://www.kqed.org/news/12094577/the-san-francisco-rock-climbing-spot-known-for-its-shiny-surface)
